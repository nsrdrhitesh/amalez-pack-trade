## Hierarchy

- Rotogravure Pouches → stand-up → zipper/window; three-side-seal; centre-seal.
- Digital Printed Pouches → stand-up → multi-design; flat pouches.
- Flexo Labels → paper; filmic → clear/white.
- Paper Boxes → folding cartons → tuck-end/auto-bottom; sleeves.
- Corrugated Boxes → regular slotted cases; mailers → inside print; partitions/inserts.

The five roots are the confirmed business categories. The 20 descendants are researched format guides, explicitly subject to project review. Their existence is not confirmation that every technical combination can be supplied. The hierarchy remains editable in the existing CMS.

## Publication and missing facts

No machinery or manufacturing section is introduced. The old Infrastructure page is retained as a draft and its menu link disabled. Existing demo services/posts are retained as drafts, preserving IDs and relationships. Company address, phone, legal entity, certifications, years of operation and service geography remain unset. No testimonials or quantitative claims are added.

SEO metadata is written for the new content, with exact canonical paths, service relationships, useful questions and visible FAQ answers. Organization schema uses the confirmed display brand and domain; no address or legal name is inferred. Global indexing remains disabled pending editorial approval, including legal/contact details. FAQ markup is descriptive and carries no promise of Google rich-result eligibility or ranking.

All bitmap artwork is generated independently using the built-in image tool and identified as illustrative. The asset manifest records purpose, prompt, ALT text, dimensions and checksum. Product images are not proof of delivered work or technical performance.
